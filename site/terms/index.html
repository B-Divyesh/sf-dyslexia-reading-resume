Terms for using Reading Resume

What the extension does

Reading Resume helps you save and return to text on ordinary web pages. It is not a diagnostic, medical, educational-assessment, or treatment service. Results can vary when publishers change page text or prevent extensions from reading content.

Your use

Use the extension only on content you are allowed to access. Do not use it to bypass paywalls, access controls, digital rights management, or publisher restrictions. You are responsible for following applicable law and website terms.
