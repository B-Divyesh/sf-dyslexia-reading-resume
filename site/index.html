How it works

Save, return, and continue reading

  1. 01

    Save a sentence

    Select a sentence, or leave one near the middle of the page. Then save it with the extension.

  2. 02
    ⌁

    Open the page again

    Choose Resume to return to the saved sentence after a reload or a later visit.

  3. 03
    ▶

    Use the reading strip

    Move one sentence at a time. Use your browser voice when read aloud is useful.

Privacy and limits

Your saved places stay in browser storage

  • ⌂

    Local saved places

    The extension keeps page anchors and reading settings in browser storage on your device.

  • ◌

    Browser read aloud

    Read aloud uses your browser or device voice. The extension does not send article text to Reading Resume.

  • ∅

    No account required

    The website has no analytics, ads, or third-party runtime scripts.

Install the extension

Add Reading Resume to Chrome or Edge

Download the ZIP, unzip it, then load the folder from your browser’s extensions page.

  1. 1

    Download and unzip Reading Resume.

  2. 2

    Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.

  3. 3

    Choose Load unpacked and select the unzipped folder.
