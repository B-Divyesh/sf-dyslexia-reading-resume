Privacy policy

How Reading Resume handles your data

Effective September 5, 2026

Data stored on your device

Reading Resume stores page URLs, page titles, saved sentences, nearby sentence context, timestamps, and reading-strip preferences in browser extension storage. We do not receive this information. Exports go only to the location you choose.

Read aloud

Read aloud uses speech tools and voices supplied by your browser or operating system. Reading Resume does not send article text to its servers. Your browser or device provider may have its own voice-service policy.

Website and license checks

This website has no analytics, advertising trackers, cookies, or third-party runtime scripts. If you buy Plus, Sociobot and Dodo provide checkout and act as merchant of record. Reading Resume sends a license token to Sociobot only when it checks whether the optional unlock is active. A successful result is cached for up to 24 hours.

Browser permissions

The extension runs on ordinary HTTP and HTTPS pages so it can find and restore a sentence. It cannot run on protected browser pages, extension stores, or built-in viewers. Storage permission keeps saved places local. Tabs permission opens a saved page only when you choose it.

Delete saved places

Clear saved places from Reading settings, remove extension site data, or uninstall the extension. Removing the extension deletes its local data according to your browser’s behavior.

Contact and changes

Questions can be filed in the project issue tracker on GitHub. Material changes update the effective date. Reading Resume is reading support, not a diagnosis or treatment.
